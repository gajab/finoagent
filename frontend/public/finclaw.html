About the Brand

FinClaw is FinoAgent

Searching for FinClaw, Finance Claw, or Fino Agent? You've found it. They're alternate names for the same product — FinoAgent, the agentic finance platform at finoagent.ai.

One platform, two names

FinoAgent (the claw in our logo is where "FinClaw" comes from) is a platform for agentic finance: you deploy autonomous AI agents with specific mandates, and they work your portfolio and the market around the clock. Whichever name brought you here, the product is the same and lives at finoagent.ai.

What the platform does

  • Agentic stock research — fundamentals, technicals, options positioning, and sentiment, continuously synthesized per ticker.
  • Quant analytics — volatility surfaces and market-implied probability distributions computed from live options data.
  • Strategy design & monitoring — build and backtest strategies without code; agents watch open positions and alert you when a thesis degrades.
  • Tax-loss harvesting — automatic detection of harvesting opportunities with replacements ranked by tracking error.
  • AI news digest — breaking catalysts mapped to your specific holdings, with the impact chain explained.
  • Privacy-first — bring your own API keys; your data is never sold or shared.

Human-in-the-loop, always

Agents research, model, and recommend. You review structured cards and confirm every action. That's the design principle across the whole platform — machine coverage, human judgment.
